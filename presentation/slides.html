"The discerner." An autonomous defined-risk options agent — where an LLM provides judgment, and deterministic code holds veto power over every dollar.

The bet

Most AI trading demos hand the LLM the keys and hope.

We bet the opposite way: language models are good at reading a market regime — and should never be trusted with an order ticket.

Architecture

Separation of powers

Scout

Code. Prices every candidate from live option chains. Builds the menu.

Strategist

LLM. Reads regime + news digest. May only pick from the menu — or abstain.

Risk Officer

Pure code. Absolute veto. Clamps size, blocks entries, kills the book.

Executor

Code. Atomic multi-leg orders, escalating fills, walks away when unfilled.

The LLM never invents a strike, a price, or a quantity.

Strategy

Sell time. Define risk.

Core book

Short-dated credit spreads & iron condors on SPY/QQQ. Short strikes 15–25Δ, $5 wings. Theta accrues whether the market trends or naps.

Regime playbook

Uptrend → put credit spread · Downtrend → call credit spread · Rangebound → iron condor · Unclear → abstain.

Satellites

≤ 2 small momentum debit verticals on mega-caps. Capped risk, capped count.

Robustness

Every failure falls toward flat

LLM fails

Multi-model fallback chain; two bad answers → abstain. No trade beats a bad trade.

Fill fails

Escalate the limit once, then cancel and walk away. No stale orders queued overnight.

Infrastructure fails

On restart, the agent rebuilds its position state from the broker. It never trades blind.
